About Ayush Sonare

Unreal Engine developer and gameplay programmer building interactive, multiplayer, and AI-driven game experiences.

Building reliable game systems from concept to release.

I am an Unreal Engine developer and gameplay programmer with hands-on experience building game systems across PC and Android. My work spans C++ and Blueprint architecture, multiplayer network replication, online services integration (EOS), enemy AI behaviour trees, combat mechanics, and mobile profiling.

In production environments, I focus on engineering systems that are maintainable, modular, and performance-conscious. I advocate for clean decoupling, utilizing Blueprint Interfaces and Gameplay Components rather than brittle casting chains, ensuring gameplay features scale gracefully without introducing regressions or garbage collection stalls.

During my tenure at CarinaSoftLabs (January 2025 to October 2026), I contributed to multiplayer titles, virtual collaborative environments, and tactical gameplay frameworks, working with cross-disciplinary teams to turn creative design goals into stable, shipped game builds.

Core Engineering Principles

01. Decoupled & Modular Design

Prefer Blueprint Interfaces and actor components over direct dependencies, keeping systems testable and package load times minimal.

02. Authoritative Networking

Treat the server as the ultimate authority on gameplay state, health, and combat while providing smooth local prediction and interpolation for the player.

03. Performance From Day One

Continuous profiling using Unreal Insights and device monitors rather than waiting for late-stage release panics to optimize draw calls and ticks.
