BIOGRAPHY & BACKGROUND

About Ayush Sonare

Unreal Engine developer and gameplay programmer building interactive, multiplayer, and AI-driven game experiences.

Building reliable game systems from concept to release.

I'm an Unreal Engine Developer and Gameplay Programmer with over 4 years of experience working on games for PC and Android. Over the years, I've worked on different types of projects, including multiplayer games, combat systems, enemy AI, online integrations using EOS, and gameplay mechanics using both C++ and Blueprints.

At work, I enjoy solving gameplay problems and building systems that are easy to maintain and expand as a project grows. I usually prefer keeping things modular and avoiding unnecessary dependencies so that adding new features or making changes doesn't affect other parts of the game. I've also worked on multiplayer replication, performance optimization, and mobile profiling.

From January 2025 to October 2026, I worked at CarinaSoftLabs, where I was involved in developing multiplayer games, virtual environments, and custom gameplay systems. Alongside my full-time work, I've also taken up freelance projects, which gave me the opportunity to work with different clients, understand their requirements, and turn their ideas into working game features.

Outside of client work, I also build and publish Unreal Engine tools and plugins on the Fab Marketplace under my brand, ZedStudioz. I enjoy creating things that solve practical development problems and make other developers' work a little easier.
