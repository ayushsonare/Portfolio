LEKH

An Indian-fantasy action RPG project centered on fluid melee combo combat feel, grounded locomotion, camera shake/warping feedback, and tactical companion AI behaviour.

Project Overview

LEKH was engineered to solve one of the most critical challenges in third-person action games: balancing immediate, satisfying controller responsiveness with grounded, weight-accurate character animations. The combat loop needed to support fluid melee chain strikes, directional dodges, and distinct hit stop reactions without disorienting the player camera.

Additionally, friendly companion characters needed to react dynamically during skirmishes, maintaining loose formation while roaming, but instantly acquiring flanking angles and coordinating attacks once hostile targets engaged.

My Technical Contributions

  • Melee Combo Pipeline: Built an animation notify-driven combo input queue that handles attack buffering, cancellable recovery windows, and localized hit traces with per-bone impact particle hooks.
  • Responsive Locomotion & Dodging: Implemented 8-way directional velocity matching and root-motion dodge rolls with invulnerability frame (i-frame) state tracking and automatic orientation towards nearest lock-on targets.
  • Companion AI Squad Coordination: Architected Behaviour Tree tasks and EQS (Environment Query System) queries for companion positioning, preventing companion path clutter while providing dependable squad cover.
  • Animation Warping & Hit Impact Polish: Tuned motion warping for accurate weapon hit registration, dynamic camera shake profiles, and hit-stop frames to deliver tactile weight to combat.

Challenges & Engineering Decisions

Tactile Hit Feedback: Standard animation collisions often feel floaty and lack punch without full physics simulation. Implemented micro hit-stops (2-frame character freeze on successful impact), procedural camera impulsion via camera shake profiles, and weapon-trail particle synchronizers.

Companion AI Navmesh Stability: AI allies frequently clipped into the player's path or pushed the character off ledges during tight combat encounters. Enforced dynamic avoidance radius layers using RVO Avoidance and dynamic crowd managers, ensuring companions seamlessly peel off when the player approaches within 150 units.
