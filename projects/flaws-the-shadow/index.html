FLAWS: The Shadow

A narrative first-person horror-shooter focusing on immersive environmental tension, dynamic aiming systems, interactive physical decals, responsive weapon handling, and enemy AI.

Project Overview

FLAWS: The Shadow is an atmospheric first-person project built to test tightly tuned combat loops within high-tension horror scenarios. The design emphasizes tactile player interaction: inspecting items in 3D space, physical blood and bullet decals that react to surfaces, dynamic flashlight attenuation, and stealth-oriented enemy AI.

My Technical Contributions

  • First-Person Weapon & Locomotion Feel: Implemented procedural weapon sway, spring-arm camera damping, ADS (Aim Down Sights) lerping, and tactile recoil curves using Animation Blueprints and timeline curves.
  • Surface-Aware Decal System: Created a dynamic physical decal projector that handles angle-based normal distortion, surface material identification (concrete, wood, metal), and impact particle spawning.
  • Enemy AI State Machine: Designed sensory perception AI using AIPerception sight and hearing stimuli. Enemies transition between patrol paths, investigation of acoustic disturbances, stalking behaviours, and aggressive confrontation.
  • Interactive Inspection & Pickups: Built a 3D item inspection system allowing players to examine quest items, rotate props in view, and manage physical inventory state transitions.

Challenges & Engineering Decisions

Responsive Weapon Handling without Code Clutter: Weapon states (idle, sprinting, aiming, firing) often devolve into tangled state checks. Refactored the weapon architecture into modular Gameplay Components, separating input interpretation from procedural camera sway and audio cues.

Atmospheric Lighting & Performance: Horror atmospheres rely on sharp volumetric shadows, but real-time dynamic lights can severely impact frame rate. Optimized shadow cascades, used stationary light sources with baked indirect bounces where possible, and bound volumetric fog quality to player distance.
